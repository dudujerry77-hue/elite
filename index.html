Our Story

About Elite Cuts

Built on precision, passion, and pride redefining what grooming means.

Est. 2015

From One Chair
to an Empire

Elite Cuts was founded in 2015 by Kingsley Obi with a single vision: a barbershop where craft meets comfort where every client feels like a VIP.

Starting with one chair in Wuse 2, we've grown to an 8-barber flagship in Maitama with over 4,200 loyal clients.

2015
Opened first location in Wuse 2 with 2 barber chairs
2018
Expanded to Maitama flagship 6 chairs, premium lounge
2021
Launched online booking and VIP membership program
2024
Named Abuja's #1 Barbershop by StyleNG Magazine
